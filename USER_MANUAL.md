## Getting Started

### Accessing the Website

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the Fashion Store URL (provided by your administrator)
3. You will see the home page with featured products and new arrivals

### Home Page Overview

The home page displays:
- **Featured Products**: Top-rated items selected for you
- **New Arrivals**: Recently added products to our collection
- **Navigation Bar**: Links to browse by category or search for products

## Browsing Products

### Categories

The Fashion Store organizes products into the following categories:

- **Clothing**: Dresses, blouses, blazers, trousers, and more
- **Accessories**: Handbags, jewelry, scarves, and clutches
- **Fragrances**: Perfumes, body mists, and colognes
- **Shoes**: Sneakers and other footwear

### How to Browse by Category

1. Click on any category name in the navigation menu
2. View all products in that category, sorted by rating (highest to lowest)
3. Click on a product to see detailed information

### Product Details Page

When you click on a product, you'll see:

- **Product Image**: Visual representation of the item
- **Product Name**: Title of the item
- **Price**: Cost of the product
- **Rating**: Customer satisfaction rating (out of 5 stars)
- **Description**: Detailed information about the product
- **Tags**: Keywords that describe the product
- **Stock Status**: Availability indicator
- **Quantity Selector**: Choose how many items you want
- **Add to Cart Button**: Add the item to your shopping bag

### Searching for Products

1. Use the search bar at the top of the page
2. Enter keywords (product name, type, or description)
3. Press Enter or click the search button
4. Browse search results sorted by relevance

## Shopping Cart

### Viewing Your Cart

1. Click the **Cart** or **Bag** button in the navigation menu
2. You'll see all items currently in your shopping bag with:
   - Product name
   - Unit price
   - Quantity
   - Subtotal for each item
   - **Total price** at the bottom

### Updating Cart Quantities

1. Navigate to your cart
2. Change the quantity numbers for any item
3. Click the **Update Cart** button
4. The totals will recalculate automatically

### Removing Items from Cart

1. In your cart, set the quantity to **0**
2. Click the **Update Cart** button
3. The item will be removed from your bag

### Clearing Your Cart

1. Click the **Clear Bag** button on the cart page
2. Confirm the action
3. All items will be removed and you'll return to the home page

## Checkout Process

### Starting Checkout

1. Go to your shopping cart
2. Review your items and total
3. Click the **Checkout** button

**Note**: You cannot proceed to checkout with an empty cart.

### Shipping Information

You will need to provide the following details:

- **Full Name**: Your complete name
- **Email Address**: Your contact email for order confirmation
- **Street Address**: Your delivery address
- **City**: Your city
- **Postal Code**: Your zip/postal code
- **Country**: Your country of residence

### Completing Your Order

1. Fill in all required shipping information
2. Review the order summary with items and total
3. Click the **Place Order** button
4. You will receive a confirmation message
5. An order confirmation will be sent to your email

### After Purchase

- Your shopping bag will be automatically cleared
- You will be returned to the home page
- You can continue shopping or leave the site

## Troubleshooting

### My Items Don't Show in My Cart

- **Clear your browser cache**: Use your browser's settings to clear cached data
- **Refresh the page**: Press F5 or Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac) to reload
- **Check browser cookies**: Ensure cookies are enabled in your browser settings
- **Try a different browser**: Test if the issue persists in another browser

### I'm Having Trouble with Checkout

- **Ensure all fields are filled**: All form fields are required
- **Check your email address**: Make sure it's entered correctly
- **Verify internet connection**: Ensure you have a stable connection
- **Try again**: Sometimes a simple page refresh helps

### Search Not Finding Products

- **Check spelling**: Verify that your search terms are spelled correctly
- **Use different keywords**: Try searching by category name or product type
- **Browse categories**: Use the category menu as an alternative to search

### Issues with Product Ratings or Images

- **Refresh the page**: Press F5 to reload product information
- **Clear browser cache**: Remove temporary files stored by your browser
- **Contact support**: If issues persist, use the Contact form to reach our team

### General Issues

- **Contact our support team**: Use the Contact page to report any problems
- **Include details**: Provide information about what you were trying to do when the issue occurred
- **Be patient**: Our team will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible
